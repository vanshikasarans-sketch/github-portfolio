"Leadership with heart & Strategy with soul."

I am a brave brand strategist who loves people and has a wide variety of experiences, from marketing to board governance. I am a published researcher, have initiated an NGO, and have spent over three years in corporate, and I have loved every single bit of it. One of the most courageous things I have ever done was moving to Canada all by myself. The experience has shaped the way I approach challenges and opportunities. I feel like there is nothing I cannot do.

I believe the strongest brands are simple, with clarity in their purpose and what truly matters to them, and I try to live by the same principle by driving purpose driven change wherever I go. I work to make every room I enter feel more collaborative, and Equity, Diversity, and Inclusion matter deeply to me. I am also passionate about women's empowerment, helping the elderly, children, and animals, and I try to contribute wherever I can.

From being part of a leading, winning pre sales team to serving as a not for profit board president, I have learned a great deal and met amazing people who lifted me up, and I am forever grateful for that. I thrive in spaces where collaboration, respect, and human connection matter. I am driven to create impact, inspire others, and leave everything better than I found it.

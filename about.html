About Me

Where strategy
meets heart.

"A society grows great when old men plant trees whose shade they know they shall never sit in” - Greek Proverb

Vanshika Saran Sharma

"I believe the most effective leaders are those who listen and care as much as they lead. Here I am, building brands with purpose, governing with integrity, and connecting with genuine empathy"

Most recently, I served as President of the Algonquin Students’ Association Board of Directors (2025–2026), representing over 20,000 students while completing a Post-Graduate Certificate in Brand Management at Algonquin College. In this role, I chaired board meetings, ensured governance and bylaw compliance, managed fiduciary responsibilities, and served as the public voice of the organization.

Prior to this, I launched the Hridya Sparsh Foundation (2023), reflecting my commitment to creating social impact and supporting the elderly community. From 2021 to 2024, I advanced from Analyst to Senior Analyst at Cognizant India, leading end-to-end bid management, crafting proposals, shaping competitive positioning, and collaborating across teams. My contributions were recognized through multiple BD Analyst of the Month awards and top-tier performance ratings.

Earlier, I gained international marketing experience through interim projects at Ogilvy, Isobar, and Britannia, and began my career in digital marketing at AlgoWorks.

I hold an MBA in Communication Management from Symbiosis Institute of Media & Communication and a BA in Journalism & Mass Communication from Amity School of Communication. These experiences across brands, industries, and continents have shaped my understanding that impactful strategy is always human at its core.

I am guided by three core values: authenticity, selflessness, and curiosity. I strive to anticipate challenges, lead by example, initiate meaningful change, and support others with empathy and care. I aim for excellence in every endeavor, recognizing that leadership is measured by the impact we create, not the titles we hold.

"If you want to go fast, go alone. If you want to go far, go together." – African Proverb.
For me, collaboration is the name of the winning game!
